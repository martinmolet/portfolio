Mobile gaming Case study 03 / 03

Française des Jeux× Devoteam

From designer to system referent: rebuilding the mobile Figma library, automating variants and shipping a token-driven light/dark switch.

Role
Consultant Product Designer · System Designer
Period
Jul 2022 · Dec 2023 · 17 months
Product
FDJ mobile app
Stack
Figma · Variables · iOS · Android
Context

A mobile gaming app with a fragmented Figma library. Variants maintained by hand, screen by screen. No theming contract: color, spacing and typography each lived their own life. Light/dark was impossible to ship without a tokenized foundation.

My contribution
Product design · Library rebuild · Tokens · Light/dark
Progression
Contributing designer, then system referent
Scope
Mobile app · iOS · Android
Duration
17 months
01
Product design

Designing and improving the flows before touching the system.

System work didn’t happen in a silo. Alongside the library work, I designed and iterated on the mobile app’s flows. A screen-by-screen audit, spotting recurring friction, iterating with product and dev.

That deep product knowledge was the foundation of the whole system rebuild. You can’t tokenize well what you don’t really understand.

02
System referent

The move from designer to referent came from proving value, not asking for a title.

First a contributing designer, then the clear entry point for every system question on the mobile app. Keeping visual consistency, tooling the design/dev workflow, guaranteeing the quality of shipped components.

One clear entry point for system questions. A team that knows who to talk to when a component needs to evolve.

No more orphans, no more silent drift. The DS seen as a team tool rather than a repository people check reluctantly.

03
Figma library

A full rebuild: automated variants, tokens, light/dark switch.

Variants were maintained by hand: for each component, as many copies as there were states, sizes and themes. Maintenance exploded with every new need. Adding a color meant editing dozens of files.

  • 01Variants automated with Figma variables
  • 02Color, size, typography and radius tokens
  • 03Light/dark switch in a single mode change
  • 04Components rebuilt with slots for flexibility
  • 05Documentation aligned with developers
100% of variants automated. One library instead of parallel copies. 3× design velocity on new screens.
04
Collaboration

A DS isn’t held together by Figma files alone.

It holds through the ongoing conversation between the designers who build it, the developers who ship it and the POs who prioritize it. Cross design/dev reviews before merge, a weekly sync with eng and product leads, documentation shared between Figma and code.

The result: components land cleanly in code, with no surprises. Fewer post-release fix tickets.
